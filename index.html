Established January 10, 1983

Founded by Mrs. A.A Vaatia, Towering Tots began with a modest team of 5 and has grown into a prestigious institution catering to over 1100 pupils.

Our Vision

To create an innovative, inspiring learning community where children, teachers, and parents learn alongside each other in a safe environment.

Our Mission

To inspire children to grip the challenges of learning, equipping them with the skills, confidence, and determination to succeed.

Admission Process

Admission to Towering Tots School

We welcome new pupils and their families into our school community in Makurdi.

Choosing a school is an important step for every parent. Our admission process is simple, clear, and welcoming, making sure your child settles in smoothly from day one.

How to Apply

Simple Steps to Enroll

Step 1

Admission Enquiry

Parents may visit the school or contact our office to ask about available classes, requirements, and school activities.

Notice: Admission procedures take effect from the 15th of June yearly.
Step 2

Application Form

Collect and fill out the admission form from the school office with accurate information about your child.

Step 3

Submission of Documents

Submit the completed form along with your child's birth certificate, recent passport photos, and previous school results where applicable.

Step 4

Registration and Orientation

Once admission is offered and registration is completed, parents receive details on school uniform, books, routines, and resumption date.

Ready to Join Us?

Give your child a solid start with quality learning and strong moral values.

Visit our school premises or reach out to our office for any questions about enrolling your child.

Where Magic Meets Curriculum

We Are About The Kids...

CLASSES

Starting school is an exciting time for your child. Our schools provide a rich, conducive and friendly learning environment and state of the art facilities for all children to learn and grow from the preparatory class to primary class.

PRACTICAL LEARNING

We do our best in creating a conducive learning environment complete with standard classrooms, science laboratory, well equipped resource centre that encourages pupils to learn independently, computer room and playgrounds for our pupils.

PARENTS WELCOME

We understand that the adjustment to School is a major change for both Parent and Child. Hence we involve the Parents in our process by creating avenues for the Parents and Teachers to come together for the educational development of the Learners.

EVENTS

The school calendar provides time for practical activities like excursions, quiz competitions with other schools, and house sports to help our pupils develop academically, physically, and socially.
